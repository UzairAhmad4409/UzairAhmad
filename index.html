Game Development

I develop engaging 2D and 3D games in Unity across a wide range of genres, including hyper-casual, hybrid-casual, idle, tycoon, simulation, shooting, and RPG games. I also develop immersive AR and VR experiences with interactive gameplay and optimized user experiences.

Multiplayer & Backend

I build real-time multiplayer experiences using Photon PUN, along with backend integrations using PlayFab and related services. My experience includes authentication, player data, cloud services, leaderboards, and other online game systems.

3D App Development

I develop interactive 3D applications with Unity, including Digital Twins, fitness and workout applications, and other immersive experiences. I can integrate advanced features such as human tracking, interactive 3D environments, and real-time user interactions.

UI/UX & Game Systems

I design and implement intuitive game UI/UX and core gameplay systems, including inventories, progression, character systems, shops, quests, animations, and responsive interfaces for mobile, PC, and WebGL platforms.

About Me

Building worlds where ideas become interactive experiences.

I'm Uzair Ahmad, a Unity Game Developer with almost 3 years of experience creating games, interactive experiences, and multiplayer systems.

I enjoy turning ideas into engaging, playable experiences—from gameplay mechanics and progression systems to multiplayer features, backend integration, and intuitive game interfaces. I've worked across genres including idle, simulation, tycoon, shooting, RPG, and casual games, as well as interactive 3D applications.

My core expertise includes Unity, C#, Photon PUN, PlayFab, Firebase, multiplayer development, game systems, and UI/UX. I enjoy solving technical challenges and finding practical ways to bring creative concepts to life.

But I'm not just a developer—I'm a continuous learner. Technology and game development are constantly evolving, and I believe there's always something new to explore, improve, and build. I enjoy experimenting with new tools, learning new techniques, and pushing myself to create better experiences with every project.

I build. I learn. I experiment. And I keep getting better. 🚀
